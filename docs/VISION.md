# WeatherGPT vision: the map roadmap

Context for future sessions. This is where WeatherGPT is heading after the SIH26068 demo builds (`demo-v1` … `demo-v6`). Each step is a separate piece of work with its own commits, tests and report entry in `IMPROVEMENTS_REPORT.md`.

## Honesty rules (apply to every step)

These come from the ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` and are not optional:

- **Every number on a map comes from an API.** Open-Meteo, GFS, the archive or a community report: never invented, never interpolated without saying so. A smooth surface drawn between grid points is labelled as interpolated from the model grid.
- **Show the model resolution.** Every map shows "Model grid ≈ X km · estimated · not official IMD". Open-Meteo blends models of roughly 1–11 km over India; GFS is 0.25° (about 28 km). A value at a tapped point is the model cell's value, not a measurement at that spot.
- **Hazards are estimated.** Words like "estimated", "model-based" and "guidance" go on every hazard surface. Nothing is presented as an official IMD warning; alerts keep "Generated by WeatherGPT, not official IMD warnings".
- **One threshold table.** Hazard colours and levels come from `backend/thresholds.py` (via `/api/alerts/thresholds` in the UI). Nothing re-defines a threshold.
- **Four languages.** All new text exists in en, hi, hinglish and mr.

## 1. Live 3D weather map *(done: `79fecb2`, `3624e30`)*

- MapLibre GL + deck.gl on `/map`, styled like Living Sky (dark basemap, gold and cyan accents), free keyless tiles (OpenFreeMap vector tiles, AWS open terrain tiles in terrarium encoding).
- **Tap anywhere** for point weather: temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind with direction, rain chance, AQI, UV, local time, with an animated 3D pin and a glass card.
- **Grid layer:** one batched Open-Meteo request for a grid over the visible area, as a smooth temperature surface with toggles for humidity, wind and rain.
- **Broadcast mode:** 3D terrain with tilt and pitch, extruded rain columns, animated wind particles following the real wind, a slow camera flyover and a large "news style" title and legend. 2D fallback under reduced motion and lite mode.
- **Time slider** over the next 72 hours that drives every layer.
- **Heat-stress layer:** a documented heat index from temperature and humidity, with categories and advice for outdoor workers, labelled as guidance.

## 2. Hazard map *(done: `5c2a42a`, `9a7d0d0`, `bc5f9ee`)*

- An H3 hex grid over the visible area. Each cell is scored with the rules in `backend/thresholds.py` (rain, heat, cold, wind, storm) for the selected time window and coloured green → yellow → orange → red → dark red.
- **Roads coloured by hazard**, like traffic colouring: each road segment takes the worst level of the cells it crosses. Road geometry comes from the vector tiles; nothing is drawn where there is no data.
- Every cell links to the chat (`askHref`) for an explanation in words. *(Not done yet: the tap card explains the level itself.)*

**As built (step 2):**
- `GET /api/map/hazard`: H3 res 6 (6.5 km hexes, the closest size to the ~8 km model cell that is not coarser), coarser when zoomed out. Each hex takes its nearest sample point's values; nothing is interpolated.
- **One scorer** (`backend/alerts/scoring.py`) for alerts, the risk board, push, CAP and the map. `tests/test_hazard.py` checks they agree in 17 scenarios.
- **Levels** none < advisory < watch < warning < severe. The severe rules borrow published bounds: IMD very heavy rain, IMD severe heat wave, Beaufort 10, WMO 99. Heat stress uses the NWS heat-index bands.
- **Roads** are coloured from the basemap's own `transportation` vectors, major classes only. The legend says it is not traffic.
- **Shared tiles and limits.** Shared fixed weather tiles keep Open-Meteo calls low, plus an optional `OPEN_METEO_API_KEY` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPTILER_KEY`. There is an offline demo map for Pune–Mumbai.

## 3. Weather-safe routes

- Route between two places; sample the route, estimate when the traveller reaches each point, and score the hazard **at that time** from the hourly forecast.
- Show the worst stretch, the time it is reached and a "leave earlier / later" comparison. Guidance only, never "safe" in an absolute sense.
- Routing needs a routing engine (OSRM / Valhalla public instances or self-hosted): a dependency decision for later.

## 4. Community ground truth

- Signed-in users report what they see (rain, waterlogging, fallen tree, heat). Reports are stored in Supabase with RLS, time-limited and shown on the map.
- **Trust score** per report: agreement with nearby reports, agreement with the model, the reporter's history. Low-trust reports are hidden by default.
- **Experimental waterlogging-risk estimate** from forecast rain and elevation (terrain tiles; low points and flat basins). Labelled experimental; checked against community reports before it is promoted.

## 5. Android app via Capacitor

- Wrap the PWA with Capacitor: native push (FCM) alongside Web Push, native geolocation, offline cache of the last forecast and map tiles for the home area.
- Same backend, same grounding rules; no logic forks between web and app.

## 6. Further ideas

- **Local-language bulletin videos:** a short generated video per district and day, built from the grounded templates (no LLM numbers), narrated with TTS in hi / mr / en.
- **Ensemble confidence:** "27 of 30 model runs show rain" from the GFS ensemble (GEFS) or the Open-Meteo ensemble API, shown next to a forecast.
- **Heat stress for outdoor workers** beyond the map layer: shift-planning advice (work/rest cycles) for construction and farm workers, as guidance with sources cited.
